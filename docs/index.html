Five IMD coins. One token.

Methodology

  • Universe. Tokens launched through IMD on Ethereum with a Uniswap v4 pool paired with IMD.
  • Selection. Once a week anyone can have the contract ask the IMD oracle, a panel of five. The question is written into the contract: among tokens in IMD's launch registries whose pool against IMD holds at least 100 IMD, the five largest by spot price in IMD times total supply (USDC, USDT and imdUSD excluded). At least four of the five must agree, and the answer is signed.
  • Weighting. Equal IMD parts at the first mint and at each rebalance; later mints buy in the current proportions. With four or more coins, a rebalance trims any coin above 25% of the index.
  • Rebalance. Anyone runs it once a new list is in. Coins that left are sold; new ones are bought. Each trade stops at a 1% move in the pool's square-root price (about 2% in price), so a large holding leaves over several weeks.
  • Minting and redeeming. Minting buys every coin in proportion. Redeeming pays the coins in kind, or sells them for IMD.

Fees

  • 0.50% on mints and on redeems; nothing while you hold.
  • The pools charge their own 1.25% on every buy and sale. Redeeming for the coins avoids it.
  • A coin with a transfer tax costs a little more while it is in the index: SwarmCity, in the current list, takes 2%, so a mint receives about 2% fewer shares of it.
  • A round trip (mint, then redeem for IMD) cost 2–5% in our mainnet-fork tests.

Risks

  • Smart contract risk: audited, not guaranteed. Nobody can pause, upgrade or rescue it.
  • The coins are small, volatile tokens with thin pools; the index can fall as fast as they do.
  • If a coin's pool stops trading, minting pauses until it trades again; redeeming always works.
  • The list depends on the IMD oracle answering. Without an answer, the index keeps its current coins.
  • Values are shown in IMD; IMD's own price moves the dollar value.
